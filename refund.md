# How do I get a refund?

Changed your mind about the Household unlock? You can get your money back in full within **14 days** of buying it. No reason needed.

## In the first 48 hours: ask Google Play

1. Go to your [Google Play order history](https://play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory), signed in with the Google account you bought it with.
2. Find **Household unlock** for Tesserae.
3. Choose **Request a refund** and follow the steps.

Google Play decides on these requests itself, usually quickly. More on this in [Google Play's refund help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637).

## After 48 hours, within 14 days: email us

Write to **tesserae.support@gmail.com** with one of these, so we can find your purchase:

- the **order number** from Google Play's receipt email (it starts with `GPA.`), or
- the **email address of your Google Play account**.

We refund it in full through Google Play, to the way you paid. You don't have to tell us why.

## After 14 days

The unlock is a one-time purchase, so after 14 days there's no refund for changing your mind. If the unlock doesn't work as described, email us anyway: you keep your legal rights to have it fixed or your money back.

## What happens after a refund

The unlock ends, usually within a day. Your Shared Spaces stay as they are, with their Members and cards. You just can't create a new Shared Space or invite people until you buy the unlock again.

Members never paid anything, so nothing changes for them.

The full rules are in the [terms of service](terms#the-household-unlock).
