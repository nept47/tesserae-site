# Delete your Tesserae account

## In the app

Open Tesserae, go to **Settings → Delete account**, check the list of what will be deleted, choose whether to keep your cards on the phone, type DELETE and confirm. Your account and its data are deleted from the server at once. Your other phones sign out the next time they sync and keep their cards.

## By email

If you no longer have the app, or your version of Tesserae doesn't show Delete account yet, email **tesserae.support@gmail.com** from the Google account you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my Tesserae account".

## What is deleted

- your account: name, email address and Google account ID;
- your Personal Space and all its cards;
- the Shared Spaces you own, with their cards, for every member;
- your membership in the Shared Spaces of others.

Cards you added to someone else's Shared Space stay there for its members, because they belong to that household.

Nothing is kept for later: deleted data is removed from the server straight away. An account deleted by email is deleted within 30 days of your request, and you get a reply when it is done.

## Keeping your cards

Delete account in the app offers **Keep a local copy**, so your cards stay on the phone without an account. Before you ask for deletion by email, you can do the same with **Settings → Sign out → Keep a local copy**.

Data that never left your phone, such as card usage and settings, is removed when you uninstall Tesserae.
