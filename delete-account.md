# Delete your Tesserae account

To delete your Tesserae account and its data from the server, email **CONTACT_EMAIL** from the Google account you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my Tesserae account".

## What is deleted

- your account: name, email address and Google account ID;
- your Personal Space and all its cards;
- the Shared Spaces you own, with their cards, for every member;
- your membership in the Shared Spaces of others.

Cards you added to someone else's Shared Space stay there for its members, because they belong to that household.

Your account is deleted within 30 days of your request, and you get a reply when it is done.

## Keeping your cards

Before you ask for deletion, you can keep your cards on your phone: in Tesserae, open **Settings → Sign out** and choose **Keep a local copy**. Your cards then stay on the phone without an account.

Data that never left your phone, such as card usage and settings, is removed when you uninstall Tesserae.
