## Who we are

Tesserae is made by Miltiadis Sitaridis in the Netherlands ("we" on this page). You can reach us at tesserae.support@gmail.com. Our trader details, including our address, are on Tesserae's Google Play page.

## What Tesserae is

Tesserae is an Android app for keeping your household's loyalty and membership cards on your phone and showing them at the till. Everything works on your phone without an account. If you sign in with Google, Tesserae's server syncs your cards between your phones and, with the Household unlock (below), lets you share cards with your household in a Shared Space.

Keeping your own cards, showing them at the till and syncing them between your own phones is **free**. Sharing cards in a Shared Space needs a one-time purchase. There are no subscriptions and no ads.

How we handle your data is explained in the [privacy policy](privacy).

## Who can use it

You need to be 16 or older. Google Play's own terms also apply to downloading the app, and Garmin's terms to the Garmin Connect app if you send cards to a watch.

## Your account

You sign in with your Google account, so keep that account and your phone secure. If you think someone else has access to your Tesserae account, use **Settings → End all sessions** and secure your Google account.

You can stop using Tesserae whenever you like. Sign out, or delete your account with **Settings → Delete account** (see [Delete your account](delete-account)). An account nobody has used for 2 years is deleted, as the privacy policy explains, unless it has the Household unlock.

## Your cards

The cards, notes and photos you add are yours. You give us only the permission we need to run Tesserae: to store them on the server, sync them to your phones and show them to the members of any Shared Space you put them in. That permission ends when you delete them or your account.

Only add cards you're allowed to use, such as your own or your household's. The cards themselves belong to the stores that issue them, and their rules still apply. Tesserae isn't connected to any store, and store names and logos belong to their owners. Whether a till accepts a barcode shown on a phone is up to the store.

## The Household unlock

To create a Shared Space and invite people to it, the Space's Owner buys the **Household unlock** once, in the app. It covers every Shared Space that person owns. Members join for free.

- **One payment, no subscription.** The price is shown in the app before you buy, including VAT.
- **Google Play handles the payment**, under Google Play's terms. We never see your card or bank details.
- **It belongs to your Tesserae account.** It works on every phone you sign in to. It can't be moved to another account, and it ends if you delete your account.
- **It works for as long as we run Tesserae's server.** If we ever stop it, we'll let you know at least 30 days ahead where we can (see below).
- **Changing your mind.** The unlock starts working as soon as you buy it. When you buy, you agree to that and accept that you lose the usual 14-day right to cancel once it has started. Google Play may still refund a purchase shortly after it was made; see Google Play's refund policy. If the unlock doesn't work as described, you keep your legal rights to have it fixed or your money back. Email us and we'll sort it out.

If we delete an account for misuse (below), we don't refund the unlock, unless the law says we must.

## Shared Spaces

A Shared Space is for people you trust, usually your household. Everyone in it can see and edit its cards, and sees the first names of the other members. The Owner invites and removes members and can delete the Space, with its cards, for everyone. Only the person who added a card can delete it for everyone; anyone else can remove it just for themselves. Think before you put a card in a Shared Space, and keep invite codes to the people you mean them for.

The server runs on a small free allowance that every household shares, so heavy automated use spoils it for everyone.

If an account breaks these rules, we may limit it, remove content from it, or delete it. We do this only when it's needed, in proportion to the problem, and we tell you why where we can (we don't keep your email address, so you're welcome to write to us). If you think we got it wrong, email us and we'll look at it again.

## Changes, and if Tesserae stops

Tesserae is a small project that keeps improving, so features may change, be added or be removed. We won't take away what the Household unlock gives you without a good reason, such as a security or legal need, and we'll tell you first. We may also need to pause the server, or stop running it altogether. If we ever decide to stop it, we'll announce it in the app or on this website at least 30 days ahead where we can, so you have time to sign out and keep your cards.

Tesserae is built to work offline: your cards stay on your phone and keep working at the till even when the server is unreachable, and when you sign out you can keep a local copy.
