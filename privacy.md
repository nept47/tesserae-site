# Tesserae privacy policy

*Last updated: 7 October 2026*

Tesserae is an Android app for keeping your household's loyalty and membership cards on your phone. It is made by Miltiadis Sitaridis. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

## Without an account

Tesserae works fully without an account. Your cards, how often you use them and your settings stay on your phone and are never sent anywhere.

## With a Google account

You can sign in with Google to sync your cards between your phones and to share cards with your household in a Shared Space. When you sign in, Tesserae's server stores:

- your **name**, **email address** and **Google account ID**, which Google provides when you sign in, to identify your account;
- the **cards** you keep in your account and in Shared Spaces: name, nickname, card number, barcode format, country, store and notes;
- which Shared Spaces you belong to, and your role in them.

These are used only to sync your cards and to show your name to the other members of a Shared Space you join. They are never sold, never used for advertising and never shared with anyone else. The members of a Shared Space see that Space's cards and the names of its members.

How often you use each card, pinned cards and your settings always stay on your phone, even when you are signed in.

## Where the data is kept

The server runs on Microsoft Azure in the West Europe region (the Netherlands). Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Google handles sign-in.

## What Tesserae does not collect

- **No ads, analytics or crash-reporting services.**
- **No location.**
- **Camera:** used only to scan a card's barcode. Images are processed on the phone and never stored or sent.
- **Diagnostic log:** kept on your phone. It leaves the phone only if you share it yourself, and it never contains card numbers.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept while you have an account. You can sign out at any time and choose to keep your cards on the phone or remove them. To delete your account and the data on the server, see [Delete your account](delete-account).

## Children

Tesserae is not meant for children under 13.
