# Tesserae privacy policy

*Last updated: 7 November 2026*

Tesserae is an Android app for keeping your household's loyalty and membership cards on your phone. It is made by Miltiadis Sitaridis. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. Miltiadis Sitaridis is responsible for that data (the "controller" under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR) and can be reached at tesserae.support@gmail.com.

## Without an account

Tesserae works fully without an account. Your cards, their photos, how often you use them and your settings stay on your phone and are never sent anywhere, except to your own Garmin watch if you choose to (below).

## With a Google account

You can sign in with Google to sync your cards between your phones and to share cards with your household in a Shared Space. When you sign in, Tesserae's server stores:

- your **name** and **Google account ID**, which Google provides when you sign in, to identify your account. Google also gives the app your email address so Settings can show which account you signed in with, but the server does not keep it;
- the **cards** you keep in your account and in Shared Spaces: name, nickname, card number, barcode format, country, store and notes;
- the **card photos** you add, at most 20 per account: a photo of the card that is shown as the card's tile;
- which Shared Spaces you belong to, and your role in them.

These are used only to sync your cards and to show your name to the other members of a Shared Space you join. They are never sold, never used for advertising and never shared with anyone else, apart from the services that run Tesserae for us (below). The members of a Shared Space see that Space's cards and the names of its members.

How often you use each card, pinned cards and your settings always stay on your phone, even when you are signed in.

Please don't put sensitive information, such as health details, in a card's notes or photo: they sync to your phones and to the other members of a Shared Space.

## Garmin watch

If you use the Tesserae watch app, **Send to watch** in Settings sends your pinned cards to your Garmin watch: for each card its **name**, **card number** and **barcode format**, at most 50 cards. They go from your phone to your watch over Bluetooth, through the Garmin Connect app on your phone, and are stored on the watch so you can show them at the till. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send to watch, and it needs no account. Tesserae sends nothing to Garmin's servers, and the watch app sends nothing back. Sending again replaces the cards on the watch; removing the watch app from the watch removes them. The Garmin Connect app itself is Garmin's, under Garmin's own privacy policy.

## Why we may use this data

Tesserae keeps this data to give you the service you sign up for: syncing and sharing your cards (GDPR article 6(1)(b), performance of a contract). Nothing is used for any other purpose.

## Where the data is kept

The server runs on Microsoft Azure in the West Europe region (the Netherlands). Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. The services that help run Tesserae are:

- **Microsoft Azure** hosts the server and its database, under Microsoft's data protection terms;
- **Google** handles sign-in, and Google Play distributes the app, each under Google's own privacy policy;
- **Garmin**'s Garmin Connect app carries the cards you send to your own watch, if you use one (above);
- **GitHub** hosts this website, which keeps the usual web server logs.

The data is stored in the EU. Microsoft and Google are certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework for any access from outside the EU.

## What Tesserae does not collect

- **No ads, analytics or crash-reporting services.**
- **No location.**
- **Camera and photos:** the camera scans a card's barcode, and those images are processed on the phone and never stored or sent. A card photo you take or choose yourself is cropped and saved again on the phone, which removes its location and camera details, before it is stored. It is sent to the server only while you are signed in.
- **Diagnostic log:** kept on your phone. It leaves the phone only if you share it yourself, and it never contains card numbers.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept while you use your account. When you delete a card, the server keeps only a marker that tells your other phones and your Shared Space to remove it too: the card's name, number, notes and other details are erased at once, and its photo is deleted. A card photo is also deleted from the server when you remove or replace it.

If you don't sign in to Tesserae for **2 years** (an app in use renews its sign-in about once a month), your account and its data are deleted from the server, as if you had deleted it yourself. We can't warn you first, since we don't keep your email address. An account stays as long as someone still uses a Shared Space it owns, so your household doesn't lose its cards.

You can sign out at any time and choose to keep your cards on the phone or remove them. To delete your account and the data on the server, use **Settings → Delete account** in the app, or see [Delete your account](delete-account).

The database makes automatic backups, each kept for about 8 hours, so deleted data is gone from them within about 8 hours too.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, have it corrected or deleted, ask us to limit or stop using it, and receive it in a format you can take elsewhere. Most of this you can do in the app itself: edit or delete cards, sign out, or delete your account. For anything else, email tesserae.support@gmail.com; you get an answer within one month.

If you think your data is mishandled, you can complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the [Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens](https://autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl), or to the authority of the EU country where you live.

## Children

Tesserae is meant for people aged 16 and over.
